Partnership

Syndesis contributed and acknowledged to the article “Towards European standards for quantum technologies” in a special issue of EPJ Quantum Technology.

SMB

Syndesis contributed and acknowledged to the article “Towards European standards for quantum technologies” in a https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-022-00150-1 . The article highlights the roadmap and use-case development on standardisation of quantum technologies by the https://www.cencenelec.eu/areas-of-work/cen-cenelec-topics/quantum-technologies/ . The article provides a broad overview of the current standardisation landscape for quantum technologies, as well as standardisation gaps and needs. Moreover, the article highlights why the involved companies and institutes are investing in standards development for quantum technologies.
