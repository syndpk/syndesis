Syndesis Launches RuralMed-AI Pilot under SMART ERA Open Call

SE

RuralMed-AI: Intelligent Healthcare for Rural Europe

Syndesis has launched RuralMed-AI, an intelligent healthcare pilot selected under the SMART ERA – 1st Open Call for Pilot Add-Ons.

RuralMed-AI addresses a critical challenge across Europe’s rural and remote regions: supporting general practitioners who work with limited connectivity, high workloads, and fragmented patient information.

Building on Syndesis’ iWelli healthcare ecosystem and the EdgeMind AI platform, the pilot delivers an AI-powered, edge-enabled decision-support system designed specifically for rural healthcare environments.

Key Innovations

  • AI-driven triage and prioritization
    Supporting GPs in clinical decision-making and patient visit planning.
  • LLM-based patient summarization
    Transforming fragmented medical records into concise, multilingual insights.
  • Offline-first, edge-enabled operation
    Ensuring continuity of care, data sovereignty, and GDPR compliance even in low-connectivity areas.

Unlike cloud-dependent telemedicine solutions, RuralMed-AI performs AI inference locally at the edge. This enables real-time performance while keeping sensitive medical data within the region.

The pilot will be deployed in rural areas such as the Devetaki Plateau, in close collaboration with local healthcare professionals and municipalities.

Aligned with SMART ERA and the broader European Digital Health Strategy, RuralMed-AI demonstrates how ethical, human-centric AI can reduce healthcare inequalities, improve efficiency for rural doctors, and create a scalable model for digital health innovation across Europe.
