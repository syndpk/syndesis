About Us

A Decade of Innovation, Built on 20+ Years of Research Expertise

Established in late 2012 as a spin-off from NCSR Demokritos, Syndesis builds on more than two decades of research experience through its people, projects, and participation in European R&D initiatives.

Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To translate advanced research into secure intelligent systems and deployable innovation across AI, quantum-safe security, distributed edge-to-cloud intelligence, and digital health.

Our Vision

A future where intelligent systems operate securely, adapt across edge and cloud environments, and support critical decision-making in healthcare, infrastructure, communications, and high-mobility operational settings.

Our Values

  • Excellence

    Commitment to high standards in applied research, technical development, and innovation delivery.

  • Collaboration

    Working openly with research institutions, industry, public bodies, and innovation ecosystems.

  • Integrity

    Responsible innovation grounded in transparency, privacy, security, and ethical practice.

  • Impact

    Focusing on deployable outcomes that address real operational and societal needs.

Our Journey

Before 2012

Research Foundations

The expertise behind Syndesis was built through long-term participation in research and innovation activities, including European and national R&D initiatives associated with NCSR Demokritos and related ecosystems.

2012

Syndesis Established

Syndesis was founded at the end of 2012 as a spin-off from NCSR Demokritos, with a focus on transforming research excellence into applied innovation and deployable solutions.

2013–2018

Growth Through European R&D

Syndesis expanded its participation in EU-funded and collaborative research projects, strengthening capabilities in secure systems, distributed computing, and advanced digital services.

2019–2022

Expansion into Quantum-Safe Security and AI

The company deepened its work in post-quantum cryptography, QKD-related technologies, AI-driven systems, and privacy-aware digital innovation.

2023–Present

Applied Innovation in AI, Quantum Technologies, and Distributed Intelligent Systems

Syndesis is advancing products, pilots, and research activities across AI for quantum technologies, quantum-safe communications, edge-to-cloud AI agents, and digital health.
