About Us

Two Decades of European R&D Excellence

From fundamental research to production-ready platforms, SYNDESIS bridges the gap between scientific innovation and real-world impact.

Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To advance European technological sovereignty through applied research and development in AI, quantum-safe security, and digital health. We transform cutting-edge research into deployable solutions that protect critical infrastructure and improve quality of life.

Our Vision

A future where intelligent systems operate securely at the edge, protected by quantum-safe cryptography, and enhance critical decision-making in healthcare, infrastructure, and defense while respecting privacy and ethical principles.

Our Values

  • Excellence

    Commitment to the highest standards in research, development, and delivery

  • Collaboration

    Open partnerships with academia, industry, and government for shared success

  • Integrity

    Ethical conduct, transparency, and responsible innovation in all our work

  • Impact

    Focus on real-world applications that create measurable societal benefit

Our Journey

2003

Foundation

Established as a spin-off from NCSR Demokritos, Greece's premier research center, bringing decades of computational science expertise to applied R&D.

2008–2015

EU Framework Growth

Expanded participation in FP7 and Horizon 2020 projects, establishing leadership roles in cybersecurity and IoT research initiatives.

2018–2020

Quantum Security Focus

Pioneered European research in post-quantum cryptography and quantum key distribution, anticipating the quantum computing threat.

2021–2023

AI & Digital Health

Developed federated learning platforms for healthcare, combining AI innovation with privacy-preserving technologies and GDPR compliance.

2024–Present

Production Platforms & Quantum Computing

Launching commercial platforms while advancing quantum computing enablement and hybrid classical-quantum workflows for next-generation systems.
