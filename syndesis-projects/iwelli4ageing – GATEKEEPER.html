Horizon Europe

Iwelli4ageing – GATEKEEPER

Programme: H2020 – GATEKEEPER

Lead organisation: NCSR Demokritos

Implemented by: Syndesis Ltd together with NCSR Demokritos

Project Overview

GATEKEEPER is a European large-scale pilot focused on smart living environments and healthier independent lives for ageing populations. Within this framework, iwelli4ageing extended the iwelli digital health ecosystem to support older adults at home through connected services, intelligent health data management, and enhanced tools for prevention, diagnosis, and chronic disease management. The project was implemented by Syndesis together with NCSR Demokritos.

Syndesis Contribution

  • Extended the iwelli digital health ecosystem with services connected to the GATEKEEPER platform
  • Enhanced the med.iwelli.com and mydata.iwelli.com platforms for management of heterogeneous health data
  • Contributed to tools for diagnostic support, prevention, and chronic disease management for multimorbidity patients with cardiac conditions
  • Supported the integration of IoT-enabled health and wellbeing services for home-based care
  • Supported the development of an open-source Info-button tool to enrich EHR/PHR environments with intelligent search and domain knowledge support

Project Context

  • Project focus: Digital health and IoT-enabled support for healthier and more independent ageing at home
  • Operational environments: Home care and remote health management for older adults, including chronic disease and multimorbidity scenarios
  • Validation setting: Extension of the iwelli ecosystem with IoT integration, intelligent health data services, and tools supporting prevention and long-term disease management
