Focus Areas

Applied R&D across AI for quantum technologies, quantum-safe security, distributed edge-to-cloud intelligent systems, and digital health innovation.

  • AI, Quantum and Intelligent Systems

    Syndesis conducts research and development at the intersection of artificial intelligence and quantum technologies, with activities spanning intelligent control and optimization of QKD systems, intelligent KMS, quantum-aware machine learning workflows, and AI methods that support quantum skills development and algorithm design.

    • AI methods for QKD system and networks monitoring, optimization, and operational intelligence
    • Deep learning pipelines informed by quantum concepts and curricula
    • LLM fine-tuning to support quantum training and algorithmic development
    AI QKD Quantum Computing
    Learn More

  • Quantum-Safe Security & Communications

    Syndesis develops expertise in post-quantum and quantum communication technologies through applied R&D on secure architectures, crypto-agility strategies, PQC migration planning, and integration approaches that combine QKD and PQC for resilient next-generation infrastructures.

    • PQC transition planning and crypto-agile security architectures
    • QKD integration strategies for secure communications environments
    • Quantum-safe design approaches for enterprise and critical infrastructure
    PQC QKD Enterprise Security
    Learn More

  • Edge and Cloud AI Agents

    Syndesis advances distributed AI systems that operate across the edge and the cloud, focusing on intelligent agents for real-time support, autonomy, and decision assistance in constrained and high-mobility environments connected through 5G/6G or satellite communications.

    • Distributed AI agent orchestration across edge and cloud environments
    • Real-time support systems for robotics, healthcare, defense, and sports
    • Adaptive communications integration using 6G and satellite links
    Edge AI 6G/Satellite AI Agents
    Learn More
